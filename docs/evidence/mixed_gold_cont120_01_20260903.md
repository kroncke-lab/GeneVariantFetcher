# Mixed-gold continuation tranche 01 (120 attempts): v2 discovery under both rules (2026-09-03)

After tranches 01 and 02 of the 49-attempt suite, the lead asked for
Gate-2-sized cohorts (~118 attempts). The unopened 49-attempt tranches were
abandoned (`mixed_gold/abandonment_log.jsonl`) and replaced by
`benchmarks/evaluation_tiers/mixed_gold_continuation_120/` (11 article-atomic
tranches of 120–121 attempts, seed 2026090302, excluding the 90 PMIDs already
consumed). This is that suite's first tranche: 120 gene-paper attempts
(APOE 1, BRCA1 1, KCNH2 21, KCNQ1 25, MYBPC3 1, RYR2 14, SCN5A 57), 384 gold
rows.

Under the registry's `reject_or_revise_candidate` outcomes on tranches 01 and
02, this tranche is a **discovery** for the v2 candidate under the primary
identity rule and under the preregistered secondary count rule
(`mixed_gold_count_endpoint_preregistration_20260903.md`, registered before
any continuation arm existed).

Root cause of the 121 misses (`diagnostics/fn_root_cause/`): 71 acquisition,
40 notation the probe cannot search (figure-only or indel/structural), and
10 reachable: five the model did not emit although the text was in its
request (`model_missed`), two dropped by the parser, two held out of the
paper lane by the trusted-identity projection, one re-attributed to a
linkage origin. Two of those are worth a future lever:

- RYR2 22677073 `A1136V`, `R4037C`, `Q2958R` are named only in a prose
  sentence listing variants *excluded* from the paper's figure
  ("A1136V-RyR2 [3 cases] … have been excluded"); the model treats them as
  out of scope, the gold counts them. A `<variant>-<gene>` token with a
  bracketed case count is a recognisable prose shape for the regex scanner.
- RYR2 22677073 `G4936R` was extracted from Table 2 (cell `G4936R b`, bare
  cDNA `14803 G>A`) and reached the DB, but the trusted-identity projection
  held it as a possible isoform offset because the bare cDNA is not a
  concrete `c.` allele; normalising a bare `NNNN G>A` table cell to
  `c.NNNNG>A` would clear it.

The largest FP paper, SCN5A 29709244 (1 TP, 33 extras), is a multi-variant
paper against a one-row gold; the extras are identities the paper reports.

Primary rule: **`reject_or_revise_candidate`**, and this time the precision
guard also fails at the bound (LB −2.60 pp against −2). Secondary count rule:
coverage criterion met (+3.0 pp, LB −1.7 pp); end-to-end MAE improved by
0.034 carriers per gold row but its upper bound (+0.083) is not below zero;
identity guard fails on the negative observed recall delta.

What the arms actually did differently:

- **Real v2 effects (three papers).** KCNH2 16155735: the folded-supplement
  shell was read instead of an abstract, recovering `P923fsX`
  (`c.2768delC`) — +1 TP. SCN5A 22677073: 11 of 12 gold carrier values
  supplied instead of 5, ten of them exact (the model's table mapping, on the
  richer source). APOE 23990795: two extras gone.
- **Model run-to-run variance (everything else).** KCNQ1 26481773's only
  variant was emitted in the baseline arm and dropped as a nameless
  substitution in the candidate arm (the model returned the spelling only in
  `source_notation`); MYBPC3 21302287 returned 42 rows instead of 46 and lost
  `c.3192_3193insC`; four papers gained and four lost single-carrier values
  the model chose to state or omit; SCN5A 24972929 gained four extras.
- **A cDNA-triggered quarantine (KCNQ1 30462975).** The candidate arm's model
  emitted `p.G45S c.133G>A`; with the cDNA present the VariantFeatures
  false-positive gate quarantined the row as `wrong_gene_residue_mismatch`
  (transcript disagreement), while the baseline arm's cDNA-free
  `p.Gly45Ser` was kept as `novel_in_range` and scored a TP. The same gold
  variant is accepted or quarantined depending on whether the model happened
  to write the cDNA. That gate, not v2, owns this miss; it is the clearest
  next lever this tranche produced.

Root cause of the 123 misses: 72 acquisition, 40 unsearchable notation,
5 `model_missed` (unchanged from the baseline: the prose exclusion sentence
in RYR2 22677073 and two others), 3 parser-dropped source-only spellings,
2 projection holds, 1 linkage-origin attribution.

## Reading

Across 120 attempts the paired difference is −2 TP / +3 FP with confidence
bounds that straddle both guards, while three papers show the intended v2
effects and every other difference is provider nondeterminism at
temperature 0. A single stochastic extraction per arm cannot resolve a
protocol effect of this size even at Gate-2 scale; the campaign's measured
count improvements (tranche 01: end-to-end carrier MAE 2.68 → 1.95) are the
one effect that was large enough to clear the noise, and they did not
generalise to this tranche's shapes.
