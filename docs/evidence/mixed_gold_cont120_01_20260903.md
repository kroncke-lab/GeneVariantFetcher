# Mixed-gold continuation tranche 01 (120 attempts): v2 discovery under both rules (2026-09-03)

After tranches 01 and 02 of the 49-attempt suite, the lead asked for
Gate-2-sized cohorts (~118 attempts). The unopened 49-attempt tranches were
abandoned (`mixed_gold/abandonment_log.jsonl`) and replaced by
`benchmarks/evaluation_tiers/mixed_gold_continuation_120/` (11 article-atomic
tranches of 120–121 attempts, seed 2026090302, excluding the 90 PMIDs already
consumed). This is that suite's first tranche: 120 gene-paper attempts
(APOE 1, BRCA1 1, KCNH2 21, KCNQ1 25, MYBPC3 1, RYR2 14, SCN5A 57), 384 gold
rows.

Under the registry's `reject_or_revise_candidate` outcomes on tranches 01 and
02, this tranche is a **discovery** for the v2 candidate under the primary
identity rule and under the preregistered secondary count rule
(`mixed_gold_count_endpoint_preregistration_20260903.md`, registered before
any continuation arm existed).
