  not a finding that Astra is incapable or can never help. Its much better

- Treat the elapsed-time figures as observations under mixed scheduling and

  the initial diagnostic's 180-second limit is not an established production

- A human-reviewed Astra pass after a properly configured cheaper call fails

  fallback benefit as established by this panel.
- The shared derived-count contract remains the next integration gate.
  Mechanically valid quotations and correct table arithmetic do not establish
